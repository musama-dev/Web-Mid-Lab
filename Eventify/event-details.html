Karachi Coastal Music Festival

Sat, Dec 12, 2026 • 6:30 PM – 11:00 PM Clifton Beach Park (Gate 3 Amphitheatre), Karachi

About This Event

Experience an unforgettable evening of live indie rock, contemporary folk, and acoustic fusion by the Arabian Sea. Karachi Coastal Music Festival brings together emerging Pakistani bands and seasoned vocalists for an open-air winter concert designed for families, university students, and music enthusiasts.

Gates open at 5:30 PM with local craft booths and refreshment stalls available inside the amphitheatre enclosure before the main stage performances begin at 6:30 PM.

Event Highlights

  • Four live musical acts performing across a 4.5-hour evening program
  • Open-air coastal seating with clear acoustic staging
  • Dedicated food and chai courtyard inside the venue
  • On-site security, first-aid desk, and marked parking zones
